Aftercare, the short version

Keep it clean and let it breathe. Wash gently with mild soap, pat dry and apply a thin layer of recommended ointment. Avoid sun, pools and picking at scabs while it heals.

You get full instructions after your session, and you can message us any time if something looks off.

Tattoo FAQ

The questions we get before almost every first tattoo.

Does it hurt?

It depends on the spot. Ribs, spine, feet and hands tend to hurt more than the outer arm or thigh. Most people find it very manageable, and we can take breaks.

How long does a tattoo take?

A small symbol can take under an hour. Detailed or large pieces can need several hours or multiple sessions. We give you an estimate once we see the design.

How should I prepare?

Eat beforehand, drink water, sleep well and avoid alcohol for 24 hours. Wear clothes that give easy access to the area.

How long does it take to heal?

The surface usually heals in about two to three weeks. The skin can take a couple of months to settle fully, so judge the final look after that.

Can you cover up or rework an old tattoo?

Often yes, but it depends on size, darkness and age of the old ink. Send us a clear photo and we will tell you what is realistic.

Is there an age limit?

Clients must be adults. Bring a valid photo ID to your appointment.

How much will it cost?

Price depends on size, detail and time. Send us your idea on WhatsApp for a quote before you commit.
